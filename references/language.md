# Language

Write for a junior developer on their first day in this codebase. They know general programming, not this product.
Use Simplified Technical English (ASD-STE100). Aim for clarity, not compliance: write as a colleague would.
Lead with the point.
Be precise, then plain. Use the accurate term, then explain it.
Define domain terms, internal names and uncommon acronyms at first use. If you do not know an acronym, describe its role. Leave general engineering terms unexplained.
Keep these rules out of the output.
For reports read at a glance, use fragments, put the action first and keep every fact exact.
